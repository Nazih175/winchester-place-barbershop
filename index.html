Meet The Shop

Over 40 Years of Traditional Barbering Craft

Winchester Place Men's Hairstyling is an established neighbourhood barbershop located on Queen Street South in Streetsville, Mississauga. Led by co-owner Aldo with more than 40 years of barbering experience, our shop takes pride in true craftsmanship, great conversation, and consistent quality.

Whether you are stopping by for a regular maintenance cut, a clean beard trim, a traditional hot towel shave, or bringing in your child for a patient, calm haircut, our focus is on making every visitor feel comfortable and looking their sharpest.
